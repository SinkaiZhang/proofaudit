# Coordinated disclosure policy

Potential defects in real mathematical claims are not published directly from model output.

1. Freeze the exact source, version, hash, and claim boundary.
2. Require a reproducible verifier or a complete mathematical argument.
3. Obtain independent expert review appropriate to the finding.
4. Notify the authors privately and provide the evidence and a fixed response period.
5. Publish the evidence boundary, author response, corrections, and unresolved points together.

`REQUIRES_REVIEW` means that evidence is open. It must not be described as proof that the paper is wrong. `FAIL` is reserved for a verified refutation or verified invalidity of the authoritative claim.
